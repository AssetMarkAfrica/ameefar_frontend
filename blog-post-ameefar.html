Welcome to Ameefar Energy: Powering the Circular Economy

Ameefar Energy is a technology company building the digital infrastructure for global trade in recyclable commodities. We sit at the intersection of technology, logistics, and sustainability — connecting buyers and sellers of recycled materials across Africa, Europe, and the Americas through a single, trusted platform.

Our name comes from two ideas: impact and reach. We believe that every tonne of material diverted from a landfill and put back into the production cycle is a win for the planet. Our goal is to make that happen at scale.

What We Do

We operate an end-to-end marketplace where recyclable commodities — plastics like PVC, PP, PET, HDPE, LDPE, and others — are listed, negotiated, inspected, and traded. But we are more than just a listing board. We are the neutral intermediary in every transaction, providing:

  • A digital marketplace where sellers list material and buyers find exactly what they need, with powerful filters for material type, country, availability, and quantity.
  • Secure negotiation tools including real-time messaging, counter-offers, and transparent bid management — all while keeping identities confidential until both parties are ready to proceed.
  • Professional quality inspection managed by our team, so buyers know exactly what they are getting before they pay.
  • Escrow payment processing through Paystack — funds are held securely and only released when both parties confirm the trade is complete.
  • Logistics coordination support for cross-border shipments, including customs and regulatory guidance.
  • Dispute resolution when things do not go as planned.

Why It Matters

The circular economy depends on materials moving efficiently from where they are recovered to where they are needed. Today, that flow is fragmented — deals happen over phone calls and emails, quality is inconsistent, payments are risky, and trust is hard to build across borders.

Ameefar Energy solves these problems with technology and a service model that puts trust first. Every feature we build, from our negotiation chat to our inspection workflow to our escrow system, is designed to reduce risk and increase transparency for both buyers and sellers.

Who We Serve

  • Sellers — recyclers, recovery facilities, and material aggregators who want to reach serious buyers across multiple countries without spending weeks negotiating individual deals.
  • Buyers — manufacturers, processors, and converters who need a reliable, verifiable supply of recycled feedstock for their production lines.
  • The planet — by making recycling economically viable and efficient, we help reduce waste, lower carbon emissions, and conserve natural resources.

Our Platform

The Ameefar platform is built with modern technology — a Next.js frontend, Django REST backend, real-time WebSocket messaging, and secure payment integration through Paystack. Every trade is documented with a complete digital trail: from the initial enquiry through negotiation, inspection, payment, shipment, and completion.

We are headquartered in Ghana, with operations spanning West Africa, East Africa, Europe, and North America. Our team brings together deep experience in commodity trading, logistics, financial technology, and software engineering.

Whether you are a recycler with containers of PET ready to ship, a manufacturer looking for a steady supply of HDPE, or an investor exploring opportunities in the circular economy, Ameefar Energy gives you the platform, the processes, and the trust to trade with confidence.

This is Ameefar Energy. Powering the circular economy, one trade at a time.
