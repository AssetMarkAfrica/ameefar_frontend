Introducing the Ameefar Marketplace: A Smarter Way to Trade Recyclable Materials

At Ameefar Energy, we have always believed that the circular economy runs on trust. For years, we have connected businesses across Africa, Europe, and the Americas with verified recyclable commodities. But we saw a problem: finding a trading partner was only half the battle. Once you found one, you still had to negotiate prices, agree on quality standards, arrange inspections, handle payments, and coordinate logistics — all without a single trusted platform to manage it.

That is why we built the Ameefar Marketplace — a complete end-to-end trading platform where buyers and sellers of recyclable materials can discover each other, negotiate, inspect, pay, and ship, all in one place. And we act as the neutral intermediary at every step.

Here is how it works.

Step 1: List What You Have or What You Need

Everything starts with a listing. If you are a seller with material ready to move — whether it is PVC, PET, HDPE, LDPE, PP, PC, ABS, EPS, acrylic, tyres, or any other recyclable commodity — you can create a "Sell" listing. Tell buyers what you have: the material type, quantity in metric tonnes, your location, and any specifications or certifications you can provide.

If you are a buyer looking for a specific material, you can create a "Buy" listing instead. Describe exactly what you need, and sellers will find you.

Once your listing is active, it becomes visible in the marketplace catalogue where hundreds of businesses browse every day.

Example: A recycler in Ghana lists 500 MT of post-consumer HDPE with MFI 0.8, available now. A packaging manufacturer in Nigeria looking for HDPE finds the listing in seconds using the material filter.

Step 2: Discover and Connect

The marketplace catalogue at ameefarenergy.com/product gives you powerful tools to find exactly what you are looking for. You can filter by:

  • Material type — PVC, PP, PET, HDPE, LDPE, and more
  • Listing type — Sell or Buy
  • Country of origin — Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, UK, US, Germany, France
  • Availability — Available now or ongoing supply

Each listing shows you the material, quantity, location, and any certifications at a glance. When you find what interests you, click through to see photos, specifications, and supporting documents.

Step 3: Send an Enquiry and Negotiate

Found a listing you like? Click the "Request Supply" button (or "Respond to Request" if you are responding to a Buy listing). This opens a simple form where you tell the other party:

  • How much you want (quantity and unit)
  • Your proposed price per unit and currency (GHS, USD, NGN, or KES)
  • Your preferred delivery terms — EXW, FOB, CIF, or DDP
  • Your target delivery date
  • Your delivery address
  • A message introducing yourself and your needs

Once you submit, the negotiation begins. Here is the flow:

  1. Enquiry sent — The other party reviews your offer. Your identity is kept confidential; both sides see only "Confidential" until a trade is agreed.
  2. They can accept — A trade is created immediately and you move to the next stage.
  3. They can counter — They propose different terms (price, quantity, or both). You can then accept their counter or make your own counter-offer. This back-and-forth continues until you reach an agreement.
  4. They can decline — If it is not a fit, the enquiry is closed and you can look for other listings.

Throughout the negotiation, you can chat in real time using our built-in messaging system. No more lost emails or miscommunication over WhatsApp.

Step 4: Agree Terms and Start the Trade

Once a bid is accepted — whether the seller accepts your original offer or you accept their counter — a Trade is created automatically. This is where Ameefar's intermediary role really begins.

Both you and the other party must independently click "Agree Terms" to confirm that you are happy to proceed. This ensures that both sides are fully committed before any money or material changes hands.

Why two-step agreement?

We have seen too many deals fall apart because one party assumed terms that were never actually confirmed. This step eliminates confusion and gives everyone peace of mind.

Step 5: Quality Inspection (Optional, Buyer's Choice)

Quality is everything in commodity trading. That is why we offer a professional inspection service — and it is entirely the buyer's call.

Once terms are agreed, the buyer can choose one of two paths:

Option A: Request Inspection

The buyer defines the quality parameters that matter — things like contamination concentration (less than 0.5%), moisture level (below 4%), material purity (above 98.5%), or any other custom specification. You set the thresholds; we check them.

The buyer pays a small inspection fee (processed securely through Paystack), and our logistics team schedules a third-party inspector to visit the material. The inspector runs the checks against every parameter you specified, and a detailed report is published to both buyer and seller.

The buyer then reviews the report and can either approve (proceed with the trade) or reject (pause and renegotiate).

Option B: Skip Inspection

If you already trust the seller or have your own quality assurance process, you can skip inspection entirely and move straight to payment. No delays, no extra fees.

Step 6: Secure Payment via Escrow

This is where Ameefar truly acts as the trusted intermediary. The buyer pays the full trade amount — product cost plus platform fee — through our secure payment gateway, powered by Paystack.

Here is what happens:

  1. The buyer sees a clear payment breakdown: product total, platform service fee (shown as a percentage), and the total amount due.
  2. The buyer pays via Paystack (credit card, bank transfer, or mobile money depending on your region).
  3. Ameefar holds the funds in escrow. The seller does not receive a penny until the trade is completed successfully.
  4. Both parties get a verified payment receipt and can track the payment status in real time.

Why escrow matters: Sellers know the money is real and waiting. Buyers know their funds are protected until the material arrives as agreed. Everyone trades with confidence.

Step 7: Logistics and Delivery

With payment secured, it is time to move the material. The seller provides shipment details:

  • Estimated arrival date (ETA)
  • Tracking reference or logistics provider information

The buyer can see the tracking information and expected delivery timeline directly on the trade dashboard. The Ameefar logistics team can step in to assist with coordination if needed — especially for cross-border shipments where customs clearance and regulatory compliance are critical.

Once the material arrives and the buyer is satisfied, they click "Complete Trade" and add any final notes.

Step 8: Completion and Seller Payout

When the buyer marks the trade as complete:

  1. The trade status changes to Completed.
  2. Our admin team reviews the trade and approves the seller payout.
  3. The funds held in escrow are released — the platform fee is deducted, and the remaining amount is transferred directly to the seller's registered bank account via Paystack.

Both parties receive a full transaction summary and can download all trade documents (contracts, inspection reports, invoices, delivery notes) from the trade dashboard.

What If Something Goes Wrong?

Disputes are rare on Ameefar — our inspection and escrow system prevents most problems before they happen. But if anything does go wrong, either party can raise a dispute. Our admin team reviews the case, examines all documentation and chat history, and makes a fair resolution. It is the same intermediary role we take seriously in every trade.
